-Adoption Process Overview-

Adopting a pet is an exciting and rewarding experience,
and we’re here to make the process as simple as possible.

Start by browsing our available companions to find one that may be the right fit for your family.
Once you’ve found a potential match, complete the adoption application. Once we recieve your application
we'll reach out via the email provided so our team can learn more about your home, lifestyle,
and experience with pets. When appropriate, we'll arrange a meet-and-greet to help you
and your new companion get acquainted. Once everything is approved during your visit,
you can complete the adoption and begin your new journey together!
Our goal is to create lasting matches that give every animal a safe, loving, and forever home.

-Foster Program Overview-

Our foster program gives animals the opportunity to experience the comfort, care, and love of a
temporary home while they wait for their forever family. Foster families play a vital role by providing
a safe environment, daily care, companionship, and valuable insight into each animal’s personality
and needs. Whether you can foster for a few weeks or longer, your support can make a life-changing
difference. Our team provides guidance and support throughout the foster experience, helping you
feel confident every step of the way. By opening your home, you’re not only helping an animal in
need—you’re creating space for us to rescue and care for even more animals.

Getting started as a foster family is simple. Begin by completing a foster application so our team
can learn more about your home, lifestyle, and the type of animal you’re able to care for. Once approved,
we’ll work with you to find a foster animal that fits your household and provide the information and
resources you need to get started. During the foster period, you’ll provide daily care, love, and
companionship while keeping our team updated on your foster pet’s progress. When the time comes for
adoption, we’ll help promote your foster animal and connect them with potential adopters. Throughout
the entire process, you’re never alone—our team is here to answer questions, provide support, and help
make your foster experience a positive one.

-Volunteer Role Descriptions-

There are many ways to make a difference at Twin Cities Animal Rescue, and our volunteers are an essential
part of the work we do. From caring for animals and helping with adoption events to assisting with administrative
tasks, transportation, fundraising, and community outreach, there’s a role for everyone. Volunteer opportunities
are designed to match your interests, skills, and availability while giving you the chance to directly support
animals in need. Whether you can lend a helping hand once in a while or become a regular part of our team, your
time and dedication help us provide better care and create brighter futures for the animals we serve.

Ready to make a difference? Getting started as a volunteer is easy. Complete our volunteer application and
select your availability. Once we receive your application, a member of our team will
review it and reach out via the email provided with information about available
opportunities and next steps. Whether you’re passionate about animal care, community outreach, events, or
lending an extra hand behind the scenes, we’re excited to welcome you to the Twin Cities Animal Rescue volunteer team!
