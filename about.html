-Our Organization's Story-

Twin Cities Animal Rescue grew from a shared passion for helping animals and strengthening the bond between people and their pets.
What began with a commitment to helping animals in need has grown into a community-centered rescue dedicated to making a lasting difference.
Every rescue story is different. Some animals arrive frightened or uncertain. Others need medical attention, behavioral support, or simply a safe place to decompress.
Through dedicated volunteers, foster families, adopters, donors, and community partners, we are able to meet those needs one animal at a time.
We know that rescue doesn't end when an animal leaves our care. It continues through responsible adoption, education, community support,
and a commitment to helping families and animals succeed together.

Our goal is simple: rescue with compassion, adopt with purpose, and build a community where every animal has a chance to belong.

-Our Core Values-

Compassion

We treat every animal with patience, kindness, and respect.
We recognize that each animal has a unique history, personality, and set of needs.

Second Chances

A difficult past should never define an animal's future. We believe in giving animals the opportunity
to heal, grow, and find the loving homes they deserve.

Responsible Rescue

We are committed to thoughtful, ethical rescue practices. Our work is centered on the well-being of
the animals and the long-term success of their placements.

Community

Animal rescue is a team effort. Volunteers, foster families, adopters, donors, veterinarians, businesses, and
fellow rescue organizations all play an important role in creating better outcomes for animals.

Lifelong Connections

Our mission isn't simply to find homes. It's to create lasting matches between animals and people
and to help build relationships that last a lifetime.
